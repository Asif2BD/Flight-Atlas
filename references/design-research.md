# Travel-sharing visual patterns

Flight Atlas by **M Asif Rahman** · [AR.bd](https://AR.bd)

Research checked 2026-11-10. These are established product examples, not a quantitative popularity ranking. Flight Atlas implements original graphics based on broad conventions; it does not copy screenshots, branded assets, typography systems, logos or proprietary code.

| Source | Observed convention | Flight Atlas adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Data and design boundaries

- All four graphics render the **same analyzed snapshot**. Layout choice cannot change totals or include excluded flights.
- Routes show approximate great-circle connections, not actual flown tracks or aircraft position.
- Points mean airports in included records, including transit. Never fill whole countries as “visited” from airport evidence alone.
- Globe views must hide far-side features and disclose the projection. Multiple hemispheres avoid silently losing a distant region.
- Journey lines represent order, not a flight between every adjacent airport. Missing links and ground transfers remain explicit in the audit, not invented connectors.
- No passport imagery implies an actual border stamp; no boarding-card barcode or ticket identifier is generated.
- Use the same synthetic ledger for the gallery so readers can compare composition independently of data.
- Keep credit **M Asif Rahman** and website **https://AR.bd** on each delivered graphic, as a small footer line on personal covers so people are comfortable sharing them. The creator is not the person whose travel is depicted.

## Future options, not implemented claims

Animated globe reels, photo journals, verified visited-country fills and region-specific covers could extend this system. They require suitable input evidence, rendering support and privacy review. A style being common in a third-party app does not mean Flight Atlas already provides that app's features.
